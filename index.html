A Note From The Counter

A neighborhood butcher,
rebuilt from the block up.

San Francisco Meat Co. picks up where Fatted Calf Charcuterie left off on the corner of Fell and Gough. The counter is run by Justin Seabridge and Kevin Nishikawa — a working butcher shop built around hand selection, in-house dry aging, and a short, honest menu of cuts, sandwiches and bottles to take home.

Seabridge grew up processing wild game on hunting trips through Wyoming and Colorado, and his father ran a farm-to-market wholesale company that supplied Bay Area restaurants and grocers for decades. The shop is an extension of that work — one knife, one case, one conversation at a time.

02 · Craft

How we
cut, age & cook.

No warehouse, no middle-mile. Whole animals arrive, get broken down in-house, and move from the dry-ager to the case to the sandwich — on the same block, in the same room, in front of you.

— 01

Whole-Animal Butchery

Primals and whole animals, broken down on the block. You see the cut you buy, and we tell you where it came from and what else is on the carcass.

— 02

Custom Dry Agers

Dry-age cabinets kept in plain sight of the counter. Longer hangs rotate through for ribeye, strip and specials — ask what's at week four and what's ready for the pan.

— 03

Hand Selection

Imported Japanese Wagyu, domestic Wagyu, dry-aged Angus and pasture-raised poultry are hand-chosen — not pulled from a list. If we wouldn't take it home, it doesn't go in the case.

— 04

In-House Kitchen

Sandwiches built from what's on the block that morning. Trim, bones and offcuts don't go to waste — they become stocks, rubs and the daily special.

— 05

Beer & Wine

A tight bottle list to pair with what you buy, selected with a certified sommelier on the floor and poured against the cut, not the label.

— 06

Classes & Demonstrations

Live butchery classes and demonstrations — poultry, lamb, half a hog — run from the shop. Hands-on, small group, with knives on the table.

03 · Our Story

Three generations
of knowing the animal.

San Francisco Meat Co. is a family-run shop opened by Justin Seabridge and Kevin Nishikawa. It took shape across two coasts, three generations, and a corner on Fell Street.

Justin learned the craft of processing wild game as a boy, on hunting trips with his family through Wyoming and Colorado. His father ran a farm-to-market wholesale company that supplied restaurants and grocery stores across the Bay Area for decades — meat as a trade, built on long relationships.

In 2023 the pair took over the former Fatted Calf Charcuterie space in Hayes Valley, kept the liquor license and sommelier program, and opened the counter the way they'd always wanted to shop one themselves.

06 · San Francisco

Hayes Valley,
on the corner of Fell.

San Francisco Meat Co. sits on the corner of Fell and Gough, on a block that's been feeding this neighborhood for a long time. We took over the space from Fatted Calf Charcuterie in 2023 — kept the sightline through to the dry agers, kept the wine license, and added a counter the neighborhood could walk to on a weekday.

We're a short walk from the farmers' market, the Opera House and the park. Come on foot, come in a chef's coat, come at 6:45 on a Saturday — we'll be here.
